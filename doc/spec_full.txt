0. Ringkasan Eksekutif
Revisi 2 memperbaiki model inti aplikasi berdasarkan pembacaan ulang sheet rekening bank. Kesalahan desain yang harus dihindari adalah menyamakan istilah Debet/Kredit pada mutasi bank dengan Debet/Kredit jurnal. Workbook menggunakan kolom Debet/Kredit dari perspektif rekening bank: Kredit menambah saldo rekening, sedangkan Debet mengurangi saldo rekening. Jurnal aplikasi tetap memakai perspektif akuntansi LP Ma’arif.
Contoh MI Darwata Sindangbarang: Kredit Rp852.000 pada BRI Tabungan berarti MI Darwata adalah PAYER/SETORAN, arah transaksi IN, dan jurnal minimalnya Dr BRI Tabungan Rp852.000 / Cr akun pendapatan atau piutang yang sesuai.
Contoh Piutang Modul II: Kredit pada bank berarti pembayaran piutang; sistem harus mengurangi outstanding receivable dan mendebit bank.
Contoh Pajak/Adm Bank: Debet pada bank berarti uang keluar; bank menjadi PAYEE, dan jurnalnya Dr beban / Cr bank.
Counterparty, direction, transaction type, account mapping, dan bank account harus menjadi atribut terpisah; jangan menjadikan kolom Keterangan sebagai satu-satunya sumber business logic.
RINCIAN MAKMUR tidak dimigrasikan, tidak dibuatkan modul khusus, dan tidak menjadi dependency laporan aplikasi.

2. Master Chart of Accounts 1100–5000
Workbook mengandung 225 kandidat kode akun 4 digit pada rentang 1100–5999 setelah mengecualikan nilai yang jelas merupakan tahun/nominal (2026, 5539, 5900). Sebanyak 44 kode digunakan langsung dalam transaksi bank/jurnal Maret 2026. Kode lain diperlakukan sebagai legacy/reference dan tidak boleh dihapus karena dibutuhkan untuk histori.
2.1. Prinsip desain COA baru
Kode lama dipertahankan sebagai legacy_code agar laporan historis tetap dapat direkonstruksi.
Nama akun baru memiliki canonical_name; perubahan nama tidak mengubah kode lama.
Akun 22xx diperlakukan sebagai allocation/fund liability/deferred-use bucket sampai mapping akuntansi final disahkan; jangan dipaksa menjadi revenue atau expense.
Akun 5000 memiliki penggunaan campuran di workbook (misalnya Pend. Buku Ramadhan dan Alokasi Ops Buku Ramadhan). Karena itu 5000 harus diberi flag LEGACY_MIXED dan pada aplikasi baru dipetakan ke akun yang lebih spesifik berdasarkan transaction type.
Akun 2500, 3000, 31xx–33xx dan akun lain yang historisnya bercampur harus melalui mapping approval sebelum posting otomatis.

3.3. Invariants database
Journal entry posted wajib memiliki total debit = total credit.
Nominal debit dan credit pada satu journal line tidak boleh sama-sama > 0.
Bank transaction IN wajib memiliki credit_amount > 0 atau normalized_amount > 0; OUT wajib debit_amount > 0.
TRANSFER wajib mempunyai exactly two bank legs: source OUT dan destination IN.
Posted transaction tidak boleh diedit langsung; koreksi menggunakan reversal + replacement.
Receivable outstanding tidak boleh negatif kecuali overpayment diaktifkan secara eksplisit.
Reconciliation yang CLOSED tidak dapat diubah tanpa reopening dengan permission khusus.
Fiscal period CLOSED menolak posting baru.

8. Workflow Bank Reconciliation
Create reconciliation for bank account + fiscal period.
Import statement and compute opening/closing balance from statement.
Compare statement movements to posted ledger bank movements.
Auto-match exact amount/date/reference where possible.
Classify unmatched bank credits/debits.
Generate journal draft for valid unmatched items.
Approve and post.
Re-run reconciliation.
Close period only when difference = 0 or an explicitly approved reconciling item remains.
8.1. Formula kontrol
Statement ending balance= Statement opening balance  - total bank debits  + total bank creditsLedger ending bank balance= Ledger opening bank balance  + posted bank debits-to-asset  - posted bank credits-to-assetReconciliation difference= Statement ending balance - Ledger ending balance

12. Prompt Agentic AI per Fase
Prompt berikut dirancang agar coding agent tidak langsung 'membuat UI', tetapi bekerja berdasarkan spesifikasi, invariant, dan golden dataset.
PHASE 0 — DISCOVERY & FREEZE
You are the Accounting System Architect and Reverse-Engineering Lead.Read the provided workbook and this Revision 2 specification.Do not code yet.Produce:1) normalized sheet inventory,2) business-domain model,3) transaction taxonomy,4) COA migration map,5) counterparty alias strategy,6) bank-side debit/credit semantics,7) unresolved accounting questions.Explicitly exclude RINCIAN MAKMUR.Any ambiguous mapping must be flagged, not guessed.
PHASE 1 — DATABASE
Implement the database schema from Revision 2.Create migrations, foreign keys, indexes, unique constraints, enums, and audit fields.Enforce:- posted journal must balance,- closed period cannot post,- receivable cannot over-allocate,- transfer has two bank legs,- immutable posted transactions.Seed only approved master data; preserve legacy codes.
PHASE 2 — ACCOUNTING ENGINE
Implement a deterministic journal posting engine.Input = normalized transaction + transaction type + account mapping.Output = balanced journal entry.Implement IN, OUT, TRANSFER, receivable payment, income, bank interest, bank fee/tax, allocation, cash transaction, opening balance.Never infer accounting semantics from the word Debet/Kredit alone.
PHASE 3 — BANK IMPORT
Implement Excel/CSV bank statement import.Map columns:Tanggal Transaksi, Pengirim/Penerima, Uraian Transaksi, Kode, Debet, Kredit, Saldo.Normalize:Kredit => direction IN.Debet => direction OUT.Do not reverse journal semantics.Store raw row and normalized row.Make import idempotent and auditable.
PHASE 4 — COUNTERPARTY & MATCHING
Implement counterparty normalization and matching.Create aliases.System counterparties:BANK, INTERNAL TRANSFER.Use exact matching first, then deterministic aliases, then fuzzy suggestion.Never auto-post a fuzzy match without approval.
PHASE 5 — RECEIVABLE
Implement receivable creation, outstanding calculation, payment allocation, overpayment handling, and aging.When bank transaction is IN and classified as receivable payment:Dr Bank / Cr Receivable.Expose matched/unmatched status and allocation history.
PHASE 6 — FUND / ALLOCATION
Implement funds and allocation buckets for 21xx/22xx legacy allocation accounts.Track allocated, realized, returned, available.Use configurable account mappings.Do not hard-code that every 22xx is expense or revenue.Require approved mapping for production posting.
PHASE 7 — RECONCILIATION
Implement bank reconciliation for Giro 304, Giro 308, Tabungan 538.Calculate statement opening, debits, credits, ending.Match bank transactions to ledger.Show unmatched items.Require zero difference or approved reconciling items before close.
PHASE 8 — REPORTING
Implement reports to reproduce workbook outputs:Neraca Awal, Buku Bank, Jurnal Umum, Buku Besar, Trial Balance, Piutang, Alokasi Dana, Rekonsiliasi, Neraca Akhir, Arus Kas, Rincian Kas Riil.Do not build reports from hard-coded Excel positions. Build from database/ledger and mapping configuration.
PHASE 9 — GOLDEN TEST & RELEASE
Load March 2026 golden dataset.Compare:- bank opening/ending balances for 3 accounts,- total bank debits/credits,- journal totals,- receivable movements,- allocation balances,- cash flow totals,- balance sheet totals.Generate a discrepancy report.Do not declare release-ready while unexplained differences remain.
